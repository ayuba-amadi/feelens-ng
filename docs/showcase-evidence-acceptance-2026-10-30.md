## FCT-IRS TIN evidence correction and proposed data review

The [official FAQ](https://fctirs.gov.ng/faq/) explicitly says TIN generation is free and separately gives a lost-TIN retrieval method; it does not give an individual retrieval price. The [official SLA](https://fctirs.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/FCTIRS-SLA-Version1.0.pdf) lists taxpayer identification number enrolment as free. The [official free-service notice](https://fctirs.gov.ng/offering-and-accepting-processing-fee-or-service-charge-gratification-is-not-allowed/) states that FCT-IRS services are rendered free and prohibits processing fees or service charges. Applying that general policy to FCT-IRS retrieval assistance is an inference, not an individual retrieval-fee statement in the FAQ. It does not establish that a telecommunications provider never charges for USSD use.

Recommend splitting the combined production service into TIN generation/enrolment and TIN retrieval, subject to explicit approval and an authorized data review. Record the FAQ and SLA as direct evidence for generation; record the FAQ as retrieval-process evidence and the free-service notice as supporting policy evidence for retrieval. Preserve the current slug through a reviewed redirect or other compatibility plan, link each new service to the appropriate official channel and fee option, and record a new verification event and actual review dates. Do not copy 18 October 2026 as the verification date for newly reviewed sources. No production record has been modified.

If the combined service is retained, add the SLA and free-service notice to the record through the authorized evidence workflow and use this proposed verification summary, with each source linked individually:

> TIN generation: The FCT-IRS FAQ and Service Level Agreement explicitly describe generation or enrolment as free. TIN retrieval: The FAQ describes the retrieval process; FCT-IRS separately states that its services are free and processing or service charges are not allowed. This supports no FCT-IRS service charge for retrieval assistance; the FAQ does not state a separate retrieval fee.

The current public record contains only the FAQ, so the full summary and its supporting links must not be presented as if all three were already attached and verified. The existing `is_primary` flag identifies the link's primary/supporting position, not a semantic evidence role; it cannot safely distinguish direct fee, process and fee-policy evidence without appropriate recorded metadata.
