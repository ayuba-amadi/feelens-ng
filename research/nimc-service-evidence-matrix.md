## Material ambiguities

- Date of birth: the charter and official fees page state NGN 28,574; the official adult modification page states NGN 15,000. The record remains conflicting with an unknown fee and both amounts in the linked evidence notes.
- Other information modification: the charter and official fees page state NGN 2,000 per updatable field, while the adult modification page states NGN 500 per field. The record remains conflicting; FeeLens does not decide whether the amount is per field or per request.
- Verification API: the charter provides subscription and annual amounts, while the current official fees page says the API is currently free for approved, registered developers. The four charter API options remain conflicting because scope and amount are not reconciled.
- Bulk verification: P4 is 20,000,000+ at NGN 2.50 per record and P5 is 50,000,000+ at NGN 1.50 per record. The bands overlap from 50,000,000 upward. Both rows remain conflicting; no upper or lower band was invented.
- The charter repeats the NIN slip re-issue row number in the African regular diaspora table. It is treated as one distinct US$6 option, with the duplicate noted in the evidence note.
- The charter dash entries for annual renewal are not treated as free or zero.

## Channel and publication rule

The catalogue publishes a fee only when the specific row is supported by the charter or a relevant official NIMC page. Individual domestic enrolment and modifications link to NIMC official service pages; diaspora rows link to the official diaspora page, which describes licensed partner operation; institutional rows link to NIMC official fee/service navigation and explicitly state that they are not individual services. Where the charter does not establish eligibility, process, exact channel or a named partner, the service detail says so rather than filling the gap.
