# FeeLens NG verification methodology

FeeLens answers the question: **“What is the official fee before I pay?”** Evidence must be traceable, current enough for the claim being made, and clearly separated from assumptions.

## Evidence tiers

### Tier 1 — primary legal or approved fee evidence

- Acts
- Regulations
- Gazettes
- Statutory instruments
- Approved official fee schedules
- Official circulars

### Tier 2 — official publication

- Official MDA websites
- Official service portals
- Officially published notices and documents

### Tier 3 — formal confirmation

- Formal MDA correspondence
- Official email or letter
- FOI responses where necessary

Agents, blogs, third-party fee websites, and unverified social media are insufficient by themselves. They may identify a lead for research, but they cannot establish an official fee.

## Verification statuses

- **verified** — the fee and relevant context are supported by authoritative evidence, with the source and checking date recorded.
- **pending** — research or confirmation is still in progress; the platform must not present the value as established.
- **conflicting** — authoritative sources disagree or materially differ; both the conflict and the sources must be retained and shown appropriately.
- **unable_to_verify** — reasonable checks did not establish an authoritative fee; this must not be represented as free or zero.
- **superseded** — a previously recorded verification has been replaced by a later source or event, while the historical record remains available.

## FOI workflow

FOI is an exception workflow, not the default evidence route. Researchers should first check primary legal instruments and official MDA publications. An FOI request may be prepared when an official fee cannot otherwise be established, when a material conflict needs formal clarification, or when the responsible body has not published the relevant information. A response is evidence only when its provenance and scope are recorded.

## Recording principles

Each fee option is separate from its service. A service can have multiple fee options, including conditional, variable, or time-bound options. “Unknown” never means zero. Conflicting official evidence must not be silently resolved, and every verification event should record the source, status, date checked, and explanatory notes.
