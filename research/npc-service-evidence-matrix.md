# National Population Commission (NPC) Service & Fee Evidence Matrix

Reviewed **2026-10-31** against current official public sources from the **National Population Commission (NPC)** (`https://nationalpopulation.gov.ng/`, `https://vitalreg-selfservice.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/`, and `https://ecrvs.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/`).

## 1. Executive Summary & Official Source Discovery

1. **Statutory Mandate**:
   - Under the **Births, Deaths, Etc. (Compulsory Registration) Act, Act No. 69 of 1992 (Cap. B9, Laws of the Federation of Nigeria 2004)**—documented on `https://nationalpopulation.gov.ng/civil-registration` and in the 15 December 2025 NPC statement (`https://nationalpopulation.gov.ng/news/npc-calls-for-consolidation-of-civil-registration-laws-to-boost-digital-reforms`)—the National Population Commission is the sole federal statutory authority responsible for registering vital events (live births, stillbirths, and deaths) and issuing Birth Certificates, Birth Attestation Certificates, Foreign Birth Notifications, and Death Certificates across Nigeria's 36 States, FCT and 774 Local Government Areas.
2. **Absence of a Standalone Public NPC Service Charter / Gazette Fee Schedule**:
   - A comprehensive search of `https://nationalpopulation.gov.ng/` confirmed that NPC has **not** published a downloadable Service Charter PDF, SERVICOM SLA PDF, or statutory Gazette fee table on its main website (`https://nationalpopulation.gov.ng/legal` returns HTTP 404).
   - Instead, official NPC public evidence consists of:
     - Official webpages on `https://nationalpopulation.gov.ng/` (`/civil-registration`, `/FAQs`, `/faq-vitalreg`, `/our-offices`, and dated news releases from `2025-01-01`, `2025-12-15`, and `2026-01-01`);
     - The unified **NPC VitalReg Electronic Civil Registration Self-Service Portal** (`https://vitalreg-selfservice.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/`, HTTP 200, last modified `2026-10-29`), whose public frontend bundles (`index-XX1KC8SJ.js`, `requirements-page-gFAnA5tL.js`, `options.helper-dw2QYx9j.js`, `deed-poll.q-DTuFytgi.js`, `reissuance.q-B1wMse61.js`) embed explicit requirement texts, age cutoffs, and free-service flags; and
     - The live unauthenticated VitalReg payment and pathway endpoints (`https://ecrvs-api.verxid.site/payments/price/{transactionType}` and `https://ecrvs-api.verxid.site/deed-poll/pathways`) queried by the VitalReg portal to display *"You will be charged NGN X for this service"*.
3. **Critical Finding on Existing FeeLens NPC Records (`USD 30` and `USD 80` Legacy Claims)**:
   - Two of the three existing NPC records in `research/production-import/batch-02-mdas.json` (`online-self-service-birth-registration` at `USD 30` and `adult-certificate-of-birth-attestation-self-service` at `USD 80`) were marked `verified` and cited `https://www.birthreg-selfservice.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/` and `https://attestation.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/`.
   - On `2026-10-31`, **both legacy subdomains fail DNS resolution (`NXDOMAIN`)**. NPC has consolidated both services into the unified **VitalReg Self-Service Portal** (`https://vitalreg-selfservice.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/`), where the live pricing API returns **`NGN 5,000`** for online self-service birth registration (`birth_registration_payment`) and **`NGN 15,000`** for adult birth attestation (`attestation_payment`)—even when queried with `?currency=USD`.
   - Keeping `USD 30` and `USD 80` as `verified` with dead links would mislead citizens. Both records are therefore **downgraded from `verified` to `conflicting`**, their official action URLs are updated to `https://vitalreg-selfservice.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/`, and both the live `NGN` portal prices and legacy `USD` claims are transparently documented.

## 8. Key Distinctions Established for Public Clarity

1. **Registration of an Event vs. Issuing/Reprinting a Document**:
   - Recording a child's birth (under 5 at an NPC centre) or recording a death (Death Notification) is **free (`₦0`)**, and the **first digital download** of an approved certificate on VitalReg is included at no extra cost.
   - Requesting **additional digital copies** after the first download requires a paid **Certificate Reprint** (`₦3,000` on VitalReg), and upgrading a **pre-November 2023 paper certificate** to a digital certificate requires **Digital Certificate Re-issuance** (`₦3,000` on VitalReg).
2. **Centre-Based Registration vs. Online Self-Service Registration**:
   - Registering a child under 5 years **in person at any of the 4,000+ NPC registration centres** is **entirely free (`₦0`)**.
   - Using the **online VitalReg Self-Service Portal** (`https://vitalreg-selfservice.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/`) for convenience (ages 0–17) attracts a portal fee (`₦5,000` with child NIN or `₦4,000` temporary registration without child NIN).
3. **Birth Certificate (Ages 0–17) vs. Birth Attestation (Ages 18+)**:
   - **Birth Registration** issues a **Birth Certificate** and is strictly for individuals aged **0 to 17 years**.
   - **Birth Attestation** issues a **Birth Attestation Certificate** and is strictly for adults aged **18 years and above** whose births were not registered (or who were born before the 1992 Act) and requires a **Court Sworn Affidavit**.
4. **NPC Charges vs. Hospital, Court, Police or Legal Practitioner Charges**:
   - **Court Sworn Affidavits** (required for Birth Attestation, Attestation Re-issuance, unattended home deaths, and Deed Poll name changes) are paid to the **Judiciary** (e.g., `₦500–₦750` on FCT High Court VARMS), not to NPC.
   - **Medical Certificates of Birth / Cause of Death** are issued by hospitals/clinics, **Police Reports** (for road-crash deaths) by the Nigeria Police Force, and **Deed Poll drafting / newspaper publication** by legal practitioners and media houses. FeeLens explicitly separates these third-party costs from NPC fees.
